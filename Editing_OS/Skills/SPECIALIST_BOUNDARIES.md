# Specialist compatibility with Editing OS

Third-party clones are read-only reference/specialist packages. Their sample
outputs, completion flags, global installers and workflow defaults do not
override the Master Prompt, Taste Profile, production contracts or Quality gate.
The complete-file register in the upgrade dossier lists every discovered skill,
template, workflow and composition with its path, hash and inspection depth.

| Specialist | Use here | Boundary / known mismatch |
|---|---|---|
| Whisper / WhisperX | Source transcription and forced alignment | Raw Whisper timestamps are diagnostic; cached words require source hash, model, language, alignment and correction identity |
| video-use | Footage understanding, visual experiments and reframing | Its own EDL/render approval and presets do not authorize production; preserve the owned EDL and selected treatment |
| HyperFrames core, CLI, animation, registry | Deterministic authoring, lint, snapshot/render | Use the actual installed CLI help and matching version; complete encoded review remains necessary |
| HyperFrames specialized workflows | Caption, talking-head, faceless, promo, motion, music, PR and slideshow references | Route only to the applicable workflow; raw timing, built-in looks, mood/media defaults or per-word stagger cannot replace approved speech timing/taste |
| open-design | Design research, explicitly chosen palette/type/layout references | Its SQLite daemon is not a the toolkit canonical store or approved orchestrator. Extract reviewed design material into readable job artifacts; don't start its database runtime from this OS |
| open-design `video-hyperframes` sample | Composition inspiration only | Its timer-driven autoplay, fixed 3s switches and neon default are not seek-safe production. Re-author through the actual paused timeline and selected tokens |
| open-design `video-shortform` | Shot-planning reference | Provider dispatch, credentials and paid generation are conditional; model names/durations need current verification; one generated shot is not a governed final edit |
| voicebox | Applicable chosen narration specialist | Verify the installed engine; a voice swap invalidates all word-timed layers and associated final evidence |
| claude-code-video-toolkit | Environment and media experiments | Global installation, cloud cost/provider calls and templates are not silently authorized; hosted Ideogram routes to the owned cost-gated engine |
| OpenMontage | Optional scheduling behind the owned adapter | Agent-dispatch examples do not authorize persona dispatch; `final_review` is not publication authority; duplicated `.agents`/`.claude` skills are separately inventoried |
| remotion-superpowers | Read-only alternative-render reference | A valid Remotion output still needs existing timing/treatment/evidence/publication contracts; do not establish competing project truth |

Historical and vendor defaults conflict in several predictable ways. Resolve them
explicitly: fixed 80/120ms padding is only a candidate; forced word evidence and
frame-safe edges govern. The owned graphics planner now preserves each selected
cue's explanatory clause rather than truncating it at a 2–4s refresh ceiling;
conflicting cues are removed when they cannot both retain their meaning. Review
comprehension on the encoded result and author bespoke scenes where needed.
Captions retain all audible words; compressed
editorial text belongs in a separate overlay. Music/ambience and idle motion are
chosen for purpose, not mandatory on every scene. A successfully rendered sample
does not establish its licensing, accessibility, native editability or readiness.

The nested Video editing engine's authored README still describes direct
`--apply-edits` as approval. That is historical diagnostic behavior. Current
production must use the owned Orchestration Adapter and consume its exact
authorization; no submodule internals were changed by this upgrade.

## New method references

Upstream drawing/window.seek/playback conventions, live audio analyzers, website controls, vendor scores and studied technique counts do not replace the native HyperFrames output clock, current forced alignment/retiming, explicit human taste or encoded readiness. No ACE-Step, Barty, Animate, Fiber/Drei/Theatre/shadcn runtime, optimizer or model is installed by these recipes. External resources are deliberately provisioned and license-checked.
