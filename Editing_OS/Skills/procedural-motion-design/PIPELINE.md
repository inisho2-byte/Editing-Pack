## Story grammar without fixed formulas

Use the causal device that serves the actual message. The following are planning
patterns, not new schema enums and not certified algorithms:

- chronology: verified time order with coherent state changes;
- one item through stations: track the item and show each actual transformation;
- question to collection: each answer adds useful information, then a meaningful grouping;
- goal and helper: obstacles and verified actions lead to the earned result;
- carried subject through worlds: preserve identity while context changes;
- process rebuild/loop: show real making steps; looping must not falsify irreversible events;
- variation study: hold comparable subject geometry to compare treatments;
- claim and proof: show inspectable evidence before expecting trust.

Map these to the existing semantic scene roles. The upstream's F1–F8 labels,
"proven" demos and strict beat/cut quotas are not local capability evidence.
There is no universal silent opening, peak hit, accelerated journey, fixed
hero or obligatory loop. Preserve a deliberate breath/held frame without
forcing a blinking prop to pass a dead-beat checker. Callback, morph and hard
cut are options whose purpose must survive the five-criteria filter.

## Three clocks and assets

Speech uses corrected forced alignment, approved EDL and `retimed_words`.
Music uses the actual licensed placed segment and verified mapping. Visual
simulation uses deterministic composition time. Record the conversions; neither
sentence-length heuristics nor a nice music grid may substitute for the speech
clock. A changed voice take, crop, source cut or track placement invalidates the
affected captions, anchors, matte/track mapping and final review evidence.

Use real product captures for behavioral proof. A screenshot on a 3D tablet
can identify an interface but does not prove the animated action is supported;
pair it with actual recording/state evidence or label the sequence illustrative.
Read source detail at the maximum planned zoom. Track each asset's source/rights,
path, hash, purpose and usage conditions. Do not automatically synthesize a
soundtrack, contact a provider, capture an unrelated account or bundle restricted
media because the upstream workflow offered it.

Sparse effects align their audible contact/peak with visible events. Keep the
selected dialogue/music priorities and measure the encoded mix using declared
LUFS/dBTP/LRA targets. A waveform/RMS plateau does not prove sound fit, silence
before payoff, intelligibility or listening quality. Preview must play the same
track/placement/mix intended for export; a different temporary score needs an
explicit draft label and cannot approve the final mix.

## Responsive composition and testing

Re-layout each requested aspect from its own content/task: subject scale,
hierarchy, caption/PiP zones, line breaks, focal crop and camera extrema.
Normalized coordinates help describe intention but do not prove a design fits.
Do not stretch/crop a portrait finished render into a landscape deliverable.
Pure crop variants are acceptable only when explicitly appropriate, verified
and not missing material meaning. Use current platform safe areas rather than
upstream fixed x/y coordinates. Unrequested variants are not implicit scope.

Before a long render, test a difficult action, dense label, texture close-up,
transition and first/last pose. Lint errors can disable downstream audits;
zero sampled text is not success. Canvas/world text needs its own actual-copy,
bounds and encoded-pixel review. Motion on twos can trigger freeze scanners:
declare justified intervals in the spec and review them, rather than disabling
the scanner or labeling every freeze intentional.

Review repeated/backward seeks and an independent repeat render under the
declared determinism policy. A WebGL driver or browser change is part of that
environment, not an unnoticed replacement. Browser DOM checks and style metrics
remain supporting diagnostics; use full decoded video/audio, actual geometry,
artifact-specific graphics QA and complete exact-hash human review for readiness.
Keep the existing visible, editable, storage-light finishing handoff and stop
task-owned preview/worker processes when their authorized use ends.
