# Scene patterns: choose the viewer's next state

These are planning patterns for the existing `editorial-intent/v1` scenes, not
another scene engine or automatic looks. Each role below is the exact schema
value. Scene duration follows the idea, speech and comprehension; refresh
intervals and platform styles are hypotheses, not quotas. Refer to handbook
Chapters 4–7, 9–12, 15 and 18 and the Claim Register.

For each instance record source beat IDs, entering question/emotion, exit
knowledge/emotion, primary job, proof/source IDs, loops, visual/caption/graphic/
audio roles, planned times, duration rationale and failure conditions in the
existing editorial contract. Use aligned source words while planning and
approved `retimed_words` while constructing. Bind the chosen treatment once.

| Canonical role | Purpose / required input | Construction and exit | Individual failure check |
|---|---|---|---|
| `stop_hook` | Earn attention with an honest promise, tension or concrete outcome | Presenter, real demo or decisive title; enter the actual subject quickly | Misleading claim; no promised payoff; decorative interruption |
| `orientation` | Give the minimum context needed for the next idea | Presenter, relevant screen or simple locator; viewer knows what/where/why | Recap duplicates the hook; details arrive before their relevance |
| `problem_stakes` | Make a real consequence understandable | Show the obstacle and affected person/system; viewer knows what could be lost | Invented fear; generic stock claims to be proof; unclear cost |
| `curiosity_open_loop` | Open a specific answerable question | State the question and ledger its payoff scene; unresolved tension has a destination | No payoff ID; withheld basic context; loops overwhelm understanding |
| `mechanism_explanation` | Explain how/why using verified relationships | Process or concept diagram builds in spoken order; meaningful parts remain visible long enough | Unlabeled arrow; diagram needs extra explanation; fabricated relationship |
| `proof_demonstration` | Make the claim inspectable through real evidence | Real demo, screen, source document or measured result; preserve legibility and context | Unsupported number; tiny proof; cropped qualifying text; unrelated B-roll |
| `contrast_transformation` | Clarify a genuine before/after or two-way comparison | Matched geometry, explicit labels and honest units; one distinction at a time | Incomparable conditions; color is the only state cue; implied fake causality |
| `escalation` | Increase specificity, pressure or consequence after setup | Add one meaningful complication; pace follows the argument | Faster cuts without new meaning; multiple competing focal points |
| `payoff` | Resolve the hook/loop with its earned answer | Show outcome and allow recognition; close the ledger | New unproven promise; reward too brief to register; unmatched opening |
| `recovery_breath` | Let information/emotion settle intentionally | Hold speaker/proof, calm motion or chosen silence; preserve authentic pause | Removed breath breaks emotion; gratuitous bed/SFX; hold misreported as freeze |
| `call_to_action` | Name a relevant next action after earned value | One readable action and truthful destination; dialogue remains clear | Multiple competing actions; unverifiable destination; CTA precedes value |
| `loop_back` | Revisit the opening with changed understanding | Match the visual/idea intentionally and resolve the thought | Abrupt audio seam; repeated footage without meaning; unfinished payoff |

## Map information to the existing graphics engine

| Graphic family | Input and useful roles | Required check |
|---|---|---|
| `stat` | Verified quantity/unit; hook, proof or payoff | Proportions only from explicit percentage/fraction; bare count must not fabricate a bar |
| `comparison` | Two defensible alternatives; contrast or stakes | Same units/context, clear labels, no invented before/after |
| `process` | Ordered steps; mechanism or demo | Each item has a valid spoken anchor; connectors communicate actual order |
| `concept_diagram` | Relationships with a source; mechanism or escalation | One focal relationship, truthful direction and phone-readable labels |
| `quote` | Verified complete quotation; payoff or recovery | Attribution and wording correct; excerpt does not change meaning |
| `asset_focus` | Matching licensed local source; proof/demo or orientation | Relevant asset, real identity, availability, crop/rights checked |
| `emphasis` / `keyword` | One decisive meaning word; any justified role | Exact word activation, scarce emphasis, stable readable layout |

Layouts (`overlay`, `side_panel`, `stage_pip`, `full_stage`, `lower_third`) are
chosen from the focal task and approved treatment. Reserve captions/platform UI,
avoid faces/proof, and review PiP on encoded frames. A `none` graphic is a valid
decision. Part/whole, change, time, spatial and hierarchy shapes may need bespoke
authoring: the planner's seven families do not prove a renderer for every shape.

## Before full animation

Build a scene map with exact anchors, reveal finish, readable settled hold,
transition and speaker state. Test a dense run plus a transition/PiP example
alongside a hero. Inspect the smallest labels at phone scale. Keep an asset
ledger in the job: asset ID, source/rights, local path, SHA-256, purpose, scene
IDs, planned reuse and any permitted exception. Count repeated diagrams/images
before animation. Do not imply rights from a filename or a successful download.

Check motion in reading order, captions against actual speech, audio/listening
continuity at every cut, and consistency between grade/background/type. Historical
project colors, typography sizes and PiP coordinates remain project-specific.
