# Generic style catalog

All entries are unapproved starting points. A catalog label never selects a production treatment. Use the actual content/reference, show options where unresolved, and record the human decision in taste-selection/v1. Do not copy a style's example status into approval evidence.

| Example family | Adjustable properties | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Clear editorial | Type hierarchy, whitespace, semantic reveals and stable captions | Proposed design vocabulary |
| Paper and printed matter | Surface grain, registration, physical folds and constrained light | Procedural illustration or verified local 3D |
| Studio objects | Geometry, hinge phases, material, camera and contact shadows | Owned Three.js draft starter, external runtime required |
| Audio response | FFT bands, attack/release, peak clearance and actual placed-audio clock | Measured reaction, not beat/lyric alignment |
| Reference-derived identity | Shape, relative palette, light, typography and motion cadence | Original observations; no automatic resemblance claim |

A job style record contains source/rights/hash, observed identity rules, proposed tokens, requested variants, explicit selection evidence and verification limits. Keep real references, screenshots, human decisions and job records in the external workspace. Promote no draft catalog entry into an approved style without genuine selection.
